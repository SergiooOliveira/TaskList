# Changes made overnight (27 Nov)

You don't need to run any SQL: the database is unchanged.

## What you'll see

### Home
- The home screen is now a **2×2 grid of big cards**: Wishlist, Books, Manga and Stats. Each card has a thin-line icon and its counts.
- The styling is plain **black and white** for now, so we can choose the look together.

### Books without a cover
- A book without a cover shows a **book outline with its title in small print** instead of an empty box.
- The same happens when a cover link stops working.
- This applies everywhere covers appear: shelf rows, series stacks, the details screen, the genre list and Stats.

### Series panel and volume panel (the sheets at the bottom)
- **Picking a book now closes the panel** and opens the book's details.
- Panels also close whenever the screen changes, for example with the phone's back button.
- **The grip at the top really drags now.**
  - Pull it down past a short distance, or flick it, and the panel closes. A short pull springs back.
  - Pulling down **no longer reloads the page**: pull-to-refresh is turned off for the whole app.

### Feels more like an app
- The installed app keeps clear of the phone's status bar and navigation bar.
- No grey flash when you tap.
- Buttons don't get text-selected on a long press.

### "Find details" finds more and merges results
Each search now asks several places at once:
- Google Books **with the author**;
- Google Books **without the author**;
- Google Books **by series and number**;
- Google Books **limited to your language**;
- **Open Library**, a second, free book database.

Results for the same book are **merged into one**. For your Harry Potter case, the title comes from one result and the cover from another.

- **Matching:** by ISBN, or by the same title and author when there's no ISBN.
- **Missing covers:** a merged result that has an ISBN but no cover tries the Open Library cover for that ISBN. If none exists, it shows "No cover", and the broken link is never saved.
- **Order:** results are ranked in this order:
  1. has a title;
  2. **same language** as the book, or as the series' other books when adding to a series;
  3. has a cover;
  4. author matches;
  5. has a summary.

  Untitled results go last as "(no title)".
- **Partial outages:** if one source is down, you still get the other's results.
- **Language prefill:** "+ Add a book" from a series now also fills in the series' language.

## Worth knowing
- Each "Find details" now makes up to **5 requests** instead of 1–2. That is still far inside the free Google quota for one person.
- Open Library's language and ISBN can come from a different edition of the same book. The ranking and your choice handle that.
- I couldn't reach Google or Open Library from my environment, so the new search is tested with fake data only. Please try it for real (below).
- The Stats card says "Reading & spending" rather than a count of books read.
